# Review pages

Some experiments were reviewed on private web pages rather than in this
repository: rendered edits with the owner's timestamped notes, effect boards,
and audits with full-size sheets. The pages hold film frames and clips, so
their links stay out of this public repository. They are gathered, with
links, on a private hub page in the owner's claude.ai artifacts, "Scene
Recall Review Pages"; a session can find the hub's address in its memory
notes.

This file records what each page showed and what came of it, so the
repository keeps the history even if a page goes.

| Page | When | What it shows | What came of it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scene Recall Dailies | Sept to Oct 2026, 37 versions | Rendered edits for review, each with the owner's timestamped notes: the Dracula rounds (v16 preferred over the real-film v17; v18 pending), the Her and Mirrors agent edits, the style rounds. | The taste notes behind ADR-0104 to ADR-0108 and the pre-publish checks in `docs/experiments/alg-mods-craft.md` and the edit-check scripts. |
